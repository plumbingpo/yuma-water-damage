Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Yuma

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm

Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
