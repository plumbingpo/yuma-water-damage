Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.

How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
