How Drying Works on a Phoenix Water Damage Job

Drying a building is not the same as waiting for it to dry. Left alone, water trapped in drywall, wood framing and subfloor can stay for weeks, which is long enough for mold and warping. Our job on every Phoenix property is to speed that up in a controlled way.

Extraction is always step one, since pumping and vacuuming remove water much faster than any drying machine. Then air movers are set along the walls to push a fast stream of air across wet surfaces and lift moisture out of them. That vapor is collected by dehumidifiers and pumped away, keeping the air dry so the process does not stall. Air movers on their own only stir humid air, so the two machines work as a pair.

A technician measures the wet materials daily against readings from an unaffected area of the property. For most Phoenix homes the target is met in 3 to 5 days, though hardwood, plaster and other dense materials need more time. We remove the machines when the readings reach the goal, and not before.

Restoration Challenges Phoenix Homeowners Face

Water trouble is not all equally urgent, and knowing the difference helps you decide what to do first. The five below are ones we see constantly in Phoenix, listed from most pressing to least.

Bursts: Nothing is more time-critical than a broken water line, which keeps pouring until the supply is cut. Depending on the season, the break may follow a freeze, and it often shows only when the ice inside melts. Close the main valve, open a faucet to relieve pressure and call right away, even at night. If a ceiling is sagging from the weight of water, stay out from under it and do not poke it.

Runoff: Rain running into the house at ground level ranks a close second. You cannot stop the rain, but you can move belongings up and keep away from any outlets near the water. This water has crossed lawns, driveways and streets, which makes it unsanitary. As soon as it is safe to enter, we pump the water out and take away porous items it ruined.

Kitchens: Leaks from a dishwasher, an ice maker line or the plumbing under the kitchen sink should be dealt with the day you find them. Close the small valve under the sink or behind the appliance and the flow usually stops. What remains is the water trapped below the cabinets and floor covering, and it will not dry by itself. In Phoenix kitchens we often dry cabinets in place by sending air through small holes in the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months. Puddles on the ground cover, dripping ductwork or wet insulation between the joists are what an inspection usually turns up. Given time, that humidity works its way upstairs and the joists start to rot. Our crew clears any standing water, brings the framing down to a normal moisture level and reports the likely cause.

Odors: Last on the list is a stale, earthy odor in a room that once had a leak, which is worth a scheduled visit. Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a material getting wet, so a leak from months ago had plenty of time. Do not scrub or spray a large patch yourself, because disturbing it spreads spores through the house. We check for hidden dampness, set up containment and clear out the affected material for homeowners in Phoenix.

Simple Ways to Keep a Phoenix Home Dry

A large share of the jobs we attend in Phoenix began as a minor fault that could have been caught. Looking over a short list twice a year is enough to find most of those faults.

Begin at the hoses. The rubber hoses on washing machines, dishwashers, toilets and ice makers wear out, and braided stainless replacements are inexpensive. Open each sink cabinet and check for water marks, a warped base or a damp odor. Check the water heater for rust at the base and drain a little from the tank once a year to clear sediment. Clean gutters and downspouts that discharge well away from the house keep rain out of the foundation.

Know where your main shutoff valve is and make sure everyone in the house can turn it. Leak sensors placed by the water heater, washer and under sinks cost little and will alert you early. A stain that spreads or a floor that feels soft is worth a call, and Phoenix homeowners are welcome to ask us about it.
