Our Story

How We Got Started

We started Yuma Restoration Co with one goal: to give Yuma residents water damage restoration they can rely on. What started as a small crew with a extraction equipment and a truck has grown into a full-service restoration company, but our core values have never changed.

Our philosophy is simple: get it right, be respectful, and always be upfront about costs. Our team of IICRC-certified technicians brings decades of combined experience to every job, from routine moisture inspections to large-scale flood restoration projects.

Being locally owned means we're invested in Yuma and the people who live here. We live here, we work here, and we're committed to serving our neighbors with the highest level of professionalism and care.
