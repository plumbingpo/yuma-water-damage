Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Yuma Restoration Co provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Yuma, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
