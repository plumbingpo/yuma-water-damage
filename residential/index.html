Water Extraction

Home Water Extraction and Removal

Storm flooding, appliance failures, and burst pipes all put water where it should not be, and having a trained crew remove it is the quickest, safest fix. From a small leak to a fully flooded floor, Yuma Restoration Co removes water from homes across Yuma. You get five things with every visit: an inspection of what got wet, heavy-duty extraction, a full moisture map, protection for your contents, and paperwork your insurance claim can use.

Water left sitting indoors threatens the house itself, everything in it, and the people who live there. Hardwood starts to cup, wiring begins to corrode, and drywall soaks up water and swells, all in a matter of hours. Wait 24 to 48 hours and mold is very likely to follow. Even a small puddle that should not be there is reason enough to call a professional right away. A technician will size up how far the water has spread and start removing it before it causes secondary problems.

Hidden leaks that have dripped for days or weeks are part of our work too. A slow leak behind a wall or under a floor can cause extensive structural damage before you notice any visible signs. Our technicians use thermal imaging cameras and professional moisture meters to locate hidden moisture pockets and ensure complete drying.

Structural Drying

Structural Drying With Daily Moisture Checks

Complete structural drying is the most critical step in preventing long-term damage to your home after water intrusion. Proper drying eliminates hidden moisture from walls, subfloors, and cavities, prevents mold growth, and restores your home's structural integrity. Yuma Restoration Co dries every home to the IICRC S500 standard, checking and recording moisture levels each day.

We deploy a full range of drying equipment for residential properties, including commercial dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers, specialty drying systems for hardwood floors and wall cavities, and thermal imaging for ongoing moisture verification. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians calibrate the drying plan to match your property's specific materials, layout, and damage severity.

Professional drying goes much further than most people expect. Even after visible water is gone, moisture trapped in wall cavities, under flooring, and in structural framing can fuel mold growth and cause wood rot for weeks. Our team checks moisture each day and only signs off once every measurement matches what it was before the loss. Depending on how much got wet, drying a home usually takes 3 to 5 days.

Mold Control

Home Mold Remediation and Prevention

Mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure, spreading rapidly through your home and creating serious health risks for your family. Yuma Restoration Co provides professional mold prevention as part of every water damage restoration project, as well as standalone mold remediation for existing growth. Our IICRC-certified technicians follow EPA and IICRC S520 protocols for safe, thorough mold treatment.

Preventive mold treatment belongs in every restoration job, and we recommend it each time. It works better and costs less to apply antimicrobials while the house is drying than to deal with established mold later. If mold is already visible, or you think an earlier leak left some out of sight, we handle the testing, containment, and removal, then verify the results afterward so your home is healthy again.

Insurance Claims

Insurance Claims Assistance for Yuma Homeowners

Insurance paperwork is the last thing most people want to face while part of their home is unusable. To make the process simpler, Yuma Restoration Co deals directly with every major insurance carrier. We record the damage in full, with photos, moisture readings, a list of affected materials, and each restoration step, then put it into reports built to insurer requirements.

Having worked through claims with thousands of homeowners in Yuma, we know what an adjuster looks for before approving coverage. Clear records and a direct line to the insurance company usually mean less back-and-forth, quicker processing, and a claim that captures everything you lost. We're happy to explain your coverage, answer questions, and advocate on your behalf throughout the entire restoration process.

Sudden, accidental events like an appliance failure, a burst pipe, or water driven in by a storm are covered under most homeowner policies. We can go through your policy with you so you know what applies and can file a complete claim. A small investment in professional documentation often results in significantly higher insurance payouts compared to homeowners who file claims without professional support.

Sewer Backups

Sewage Backup Cleanup for Homeowners

When a sewer line backs up into the house, you are dealing with one of the most hazardous water losses there is. Category 3 black water contains bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens that pose serious health risks. To clean up sewage safely, Yuma Restoration Co sends technicians with the right training and gear who work under strict IICRC protocols on every contaminated water job.

Our sewage cleanup process includes containment of the affected area, safe extraction of contaminated water, removal of unsalvageable materials, thorough antimicrobial treatment, complete structural drying, and post-restoration testing to verify your home is safe. We handle the entire process from start to finish so you and your family can return to a clean, healthy home as quickly as possible.
